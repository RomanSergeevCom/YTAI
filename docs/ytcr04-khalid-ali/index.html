Core Narrative — Story Arc
The viewer's #1 question in 2026: "Is Dubai still safe? Should I go there?"
Khalid answers it better than anyone — he survived instability in Sudan, chose Dubai as his bet on stability, built a life here. Now conflict surrounds the region again. His story is the answer.
Sudan — instability → Dubai 2013 — survival → Growth — broker → Family — committed → Feb 28 conflict → What now?
Problem
Sudan civil war. Lost stability. Family at risk. Arrived in Dubai with nothing — call center, survival mode, no network, no money. Now: regional conflict returns. Fear in the market.
Action
Bet everything on Dubai. Pivoted to real estate. Built audience organically (2.2M TikTok). Learned the market from the inside. Stayed when others talked about leaving. Wife pregnant — doubled down.
Outcome
Top broker in Dubai. Built a life he couldn't have imagined in 2013. Family rooted here. Has the credibility to answer "Is it safe?" — because he's been through worse and chose to stay.

01
Sudan — Where You Came From

Viewer need: Understand who he was before Dubai — so they can measure the journey. Sudan itself lived through war. This already rhymes with the conflict theme. This block establishes the PROBLEM in the P-A-O arc.
  • Tell me about your life in Sudan. What was it like growing up there?
  • When did you first think "I want to leave"? What happened?
  • Why Dubai? Why not Europe or America?
  • What did you imagine Dubai would be like — and what was it actually like when you got here?
  • What did your family say when you told them you're leaving?

Post-Production — Editing Strategy
Title A/B Test Plan
Test 1: Emotion vs. Info — "He Left a War Zone..." vs "Is Dubai Still Safe?"
Test 2: Name recognition — with "Khalid Ali" vs without
Test 3: Timeframe — "2026" specific vs timeless
Upload with Title A. After 48h, check CTR. If <5%, swap to Title B. Split-test thumbnails from day 1.
Emotion-Logic Edit Pattern
Blocks 1-2: Emotion-heavy — story, face close-ups, slow pacing, minimal text overlay
Block 3: Transition — story → proof. Phone stats on screen, property walk = visual demo
Block 5: Climax — tightest cuts, driving shots, serious tone. This is the clip reel.
Block 6: Logic-heavy — title cards for frameworks, on-screen data if available
Block 7: Emotional close — horse club visuals, wide shots, let him breathe
