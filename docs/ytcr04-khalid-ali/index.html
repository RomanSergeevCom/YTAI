01
Sudan — Where You Came From
Past · Context · Setting the stakes
👁 Viewer need: Understand who he was before Dubai — so they can measure the journey. Sudan itself lived through war. This already rhymes with the conflict theme.
  • Tell me about your life in Sudan. What was it like growing up there?
  • When did you first think "I want to leave"? What happened?
  • Why Dubai? Why not Europe or America?
  • What did you imagine Dubai would be like — and what was it actually like when you got here?
  • What did your family say when you told them you're leaving?
B-Roll — ApartmentThoughtful face, window light. Slow zoom on hands while he talks. City view as backdrop.
02
First Years — Survival (2013–2017)
Personal story · Hardship · The real cost
👁 Viewer need: Everyone who thinks about moving to Dubai fears exactly this period. Show them — it's hard, it's normal, people get through it.
  • Take me back to your first year in Dubai. What was the hardest thing?
  • You worked in a call center, then marketing. What did you learn from those jobs?
  • Did you ever want to go back home? What stopped you?
  • Were you saving any money — or just trying to survive?
  • How did those years change you as a person?
B-Roll — Apartment / StreetWalking shot. Close-up of face during an honest moment. City hustle — people, traffic.
03
The Move Into Real Estate
Career shift · Learning curve · First wins
👁 Viewer need: Brokers and aspiring brokers are watching this. They want to know: how does someone with no background break into Dubai real estate — and actually make it?
  • How did you get into real estate? Was it planned or it just happened?
  • What was the hardest thing to learn as a new broker?
  • Do you remember your first deal? Tell me about it.
  • What did you do differently from other brokers?
  • When did you think "OK, this is my thing, I'm staying"?
  • You have millions of followers on TikTok and YouTube. How did social media change your business?
B-Roll — Venue / Office SpaceWalking through a property. Looking at his phone — social stats. Professional, confident posture.
04
Dubai as a Choice — What It Gave You
Values · Life quality · Commitment
👁 Viewer need: The honest answer to "Is it worth it?" — through real experience, not a sales pitch. And the baby coming makes this deeply personal.
  • What did Dubai give you that you couldn't get anywhere else?
  • Your baby is coming soon. You're building a family here. Was that a big decision — to stay?
  • Forget money for a second — how does your life feel now compared to before?
  • What surprised you about Dubai that you didn't expect when you first came?
B-Roll — ApartmentRelaxed, warm mood. Window light. Maybe a personal item — something from Sudan vs. Dubai life now.
05
February 28 — Conflict & Real Talk
Tension · Honesty · The question everyone has
👁 Viewer need: This is what everyone is thinking but no one is saying out loud. Khalid is the perfect voice — he's from a country that had a real war. He chose Dubai. What does he actually think now?
  • When the conflict started on February 28 — what was the first thing you felt?
  • Did anything actually change in Dubai? On the street, in daily life?
  • What are your clients saying? Are investors worried?
  • You have a baby on the way. Are you scared? How do you think about the risk?
  • You grew up in Sudan — you've seen real war. How does this compare?
  • Some people want to leave. What would you tell them?
B-Roll — Car / StreetDriving, city passing outside. Serious, direct face. This section needs honest energy — no smiling shots here.
06
Opportunity in Turbulence
Market reality · Broker insight · Turning fear into clarity
👁 Viewer need: Flip the fear into understanding. Show that turbulence is not the end — often it's when the smartest moves happen. This is the insight block.
  • Right now — are investors leaving Dubai or coming in?
  • Why does Dubai get more money when the world gets crazy, not less?
  • As a broker — is it harder now, or are new deals showing up?
  • Who's buying property now? Is it different from a year ago?
  • If someone has money right now and is thinking about Dubai real estate — what do you honestly tell them?
B-Roll — Street / VenueProperty walkthrough. City energy. Positive, forward-looking. Dubai skyline, construction cranes — the city still building.
07
Final — Your Honest Advice
Closing · Actionable · From the man who lived it
👁 Viewer need: A direct answer to the main question — from someone who has actually lived every version of this story. Leave them with something they can act on.
  • Your best friend calls you today and says "should I move to Dubai?" What do you say?
  • If you could talk to yourself in 2013 — the day you landed — what would you tell him?
  • Buying property in Dubai in 2026 — smart or not?
  • Where do you see yourself in five years?
