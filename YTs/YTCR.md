# Core Realty Dubai
> Channel ID: YTCR

## Overview
Educational and lifestyle YouTube channel focused on Dubai real estate for high-performing brokers and international property investors. Founded to support Core Realty — an international club of elite real estate brokers operating since 2022 under the license of INTRUST Real Estate L.L.C. Serves as a brand awareness engine, broker recruitment funnel, and trust-building platform for investors exploring the Dubai property market. Goal: 10,000+ subscribers and 500K+ total views within the first 18 months.

## Target Audience
- **Geography:** USA, Europe (UK, Germany, France, CIS countries), Asia, Australia — English-speaking investors and real estate professionals worldwide
- **Who:**
  - Experienced real estate brokers earning 500,000+ AED/year considering relocation to Dubai
  - International property investors evaluating Dubai as a portfolio diversification market
  - High-net-worth individuals seeking Golden Visa through real estate investment
  - Real estate professionals exploring AI-powered brokerage tools and CRM systems
  - Entrepreneurs and digital nomads interested in Dubai's tax-free income environment
- **Pain points:**
  - "How do I start as a real estate broker in Dubai?" — licensing, RERA certification, choosing a brokerage
  - "Which areas in Dubai give the best ROI?" — off-plan vs ready, rental yields, capital appreciation
  - "How do I join an elite brokerage network?" — what to look for, commission splits, support systems
  - "What tech tools do top brokers use?" — AI ERP, lead management, call tracking, CRM platforms
  - "Is Dubai real estate still a good investment?" — market cycles, oversupply myths, regulatory changes
  - "How do I get a Golden Visa through property?" — AED 2M threshold, requirements, timeline
  - "What mistakes do new brokers make in Dubai?" — common pitfalls, client management, legal compliance

## Content Format
- **Primary format:** Expert interviews (primary), property tours, market analysis, broker lifestyle vlogs
- **Typical duration:** 45–75 min (interviews), 10–20 min (market updates & property tours), 5–10 min (shorts/tips)
- **Number of speakers:** 2 (host + expert guest) for interviews; 1 (host) for tours and market updates
- **Language:** EN

## Style & Tone
- **Delivery:** Expert, aspirational, transparent — showcase real broker success stories, not sales pitches
- **Editing pace:** Dynamic — mix of talking head, property b-roll, data overlays, and on-location footage
- **B-Roll:** Dubai skyline, luxury property walkthroughs, office tours (Core Realty coworking space & photo zones), community events (brunches, award ceremonies, after-parties)

## Unique Patterns
- Broker success stories are the hero format — real agents sharing how they reached 500K+ AED income
- Trust through exclusivity: Core Realty only accepts top-performing brokers, which creates aspirational content
- AI & tech focus: showcasing the AI ERP system (lead management, object map, AI call tracking, investment calculators) as a differentiator
- Community-driven content: monthly reporting events, awards ceremonies, sports events, and brunches provide natural video material
- Market intelligence: data-driven analysis of Dubai property trends, off-plan launches, and area comparisons
- Pre-qualified leads: viewers arrive having consumed 30–60+ min of high-value content before reaching out
- Evergreen + timely mix: foundational "how to" guides paired with monthly market update series
