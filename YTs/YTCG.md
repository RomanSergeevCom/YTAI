# Connect Group Dubai
> Channel ID: YTCG

## Overview
Educational YouTube channel focused on business setup in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Founded to support the consulting company Connect Group (established 2006). Serves as content hub and lead generation engine for international entrepreneurs. 4,000+ subscribers, 230K+ total views, 220+ videos published.

## Target Audience
- **География:** USA, Europe (UK, Germany, France), Asia, Australia — English-speaking entrepreneurs worldwide
- **Кто:**
  - Entrepreneurs considering business relocation to UAE/Saudi for tax optimization
  - Online business owners & digital nomads seeking legal residency
  - Startup founders exploring MENA expansion
  - Investors evaluating real estate and Vision 2030 projects
  - Business consultants advising on international company formation
- **Болевые точки:**
  - "How do I register a company step by step?" — actionable guidance, not marketing fluff
  - "What does it really cost?" — total first-year expenses including hidden fees
  - "Free Zone vs Mainland — which should I choose?" — ownership, restrictions, visas
  - "What mistakes do people make?" — learning from others' expensive errors
  - "How do I open a bank account?" — navigating 61+ UAE banks
  - "What are the real tax obligations?" — Corporate Tax 9%, VAT 5%, compliance
  - "How do I get a visa and residency?" — investor, employee, golden visas

## Content Format
- **Основной формат:** Expert interviews (primary), documentary series, tutorials
- **Типичная длительность:** 60-90 min (interviews), 15-30 min (tutorials)
- **Количество спикеров:** 2 (host + expert guest)
- **Язык:** EN

## Style & Tone
- **Подача:** экспертная, educational, transparent — no sales pitches
- **Темп монтажа:** средний — conversational pace, allow depth
- **B-Roll:** on-location footage (free zones, offices), screen recordings (processes)

## Unique Patterns
- Expert interviews are the hero format — tax consultants, lawyers, free zone reps
- Trust through transparency: honest discussion of downsides, not just selling
- Pre-qualified leads: viewers arrive having consumed 30-60+ min of content
- Evergreen content: videos rank for high-intent keywords for years
- Content created with UAE government entities and free zones as partners

## Key Metrics
- Subscribers: 4,000+
- Total views: 230K+
- Best performing: IFZA Free Zone interview — 40K views, 1,000+ leads
- Business result: 3x increase in inquiries compared to previous marketing
