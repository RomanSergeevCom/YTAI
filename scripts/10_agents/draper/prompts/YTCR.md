# YTCR — Core Realty Dubai overrides

## Audience voice

International broker reading English at a coffee in Dubai (or considering relocation
to Dubai). They earn 500K+ AED/year already or aspire to. They're cynical about
"how to" gurus and respect concrete deals + numbers. They check the speaker's
license / track record before they finish the first sentence.

## Hook patterns that work for THIS audience

- "I made $X on a deal that everyone told me was bubble" — contradiction + number
- "Why I left [established brokerage] for Core Realty" — first-person reveal, peer story
- "Q3 numbers: [specific neighborhood] vs [specific neighborhood]" — number-driven analysis
- "Brokers don't talk about this fee" — contrarian-with-evidence

## Hook patterns that fail

- "Top 5 areas in Dubai" — broad listicle, broker audience filters this
- "Everything you need to know about Golden Visa" — they already know basics
- "Real estate in Dubai EXPLAINED" — too 101-level

## Standard hashtags

```
#DubaiRealEstate #CoreRealty #BrokerLife #PropertyInvestment #UAE #GoldenVisa
```

Mix 3–5 per video. Don't dump all six.

## Forbidden for this brand

- "Easy money" / "Quick flip" — Core Realty positions on quality, not speed
- "Anyone can do this" — opposite of broker-quality positioning
- Emoji in title or first description line
- "Beginner's guide" — audience is not beginner
- Generic skyline thumbnails (Burj Khalifa with text overlay) — every Dubai
  channel has these; pick a specific person or specific neighborhood

## Tone notes

- "Real estate" in title is fine but "real estate broker" is stronger
- Specific area names (Palm Jumeirah, JLT, JVC, Business Bay, Dubai Hills,
  Marina) > generic "Dubai"
- Dollar amounts > AED amounts in title for international audience; AED is
  fine in description body
- First-name basis for guests when they're known broker community figures
  (Max, Ivan, Alex) — adds authenticity for the in-group viewer
