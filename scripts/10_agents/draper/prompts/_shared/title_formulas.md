# Title formulas — pick one per variant

Five proven structures. Don't mix. One formula per title. Five titles total —
each using a different formula — so Roman can compare apples to apples.

## 1. Contradiction

**Structure:** [State A] → [State B that contradicts A]

**When to use:** transcript contains a genuine reversal. Speaker hated → loved.
Failed → succeeded. Believed → changed mind.

**Examples:**
- "Я ненавидел Дубай. Потом продал на $2.2M"
- "5 лет назад он жил в палатке. Сейчас управляет $40M"
- "Все говорили 'bubble'. Я купил. Что произошло"

**Trap:** If the contradiction is rhetorical, not factual, kill it.

## 2. Specific number

**Structure:** [Number] [unit] [unexpected payoff]

**When to use:** there's a real number in the transcript — revenue, time,
count, percentage — that's surprising at first glance.

**Examples:**
- "Net ROI 25-35% — стратегия офисов, которой не учат брокеров"
- "47 минут до Golden Visa: пошагово"
- "$3,000 в JLT vs 20,000 на Palm — почему я переехал"

**Trap:** Round numbers feel made-up. Use what the speaker actually said.

## 3. Curiosity gap

**Structure:** [Specific situation] → [unstated outcome that demands resolution]

**When to use:** the story has a turning point that's intriguing without
spoiling the payoff.

**Examples:**
- "CEO открыл офис в Дубае. Я не хотел переезжать. Что он сделал"
- "Алекс никогда не верил брокерам. Это интервью изменило его мнение"
- "Polиция остановила нас в пустыне. Дальше начался другой Дубай"

**Trap:** Don't promise drama the video doesn't deliver. The gap closes
inside the video or the title is broken.

## 4. Direct contrarian claim

**Structure:** [Conventional wisdom] / [Direct denial with evidence]

**When to use:** speaker takes a hard position against mainstream narrative.

**Examples:**
- "Networking > Cold Calls — почему 50 звонков в день не работают"
- "Quality > Quantity — почему мои 5 клиентов лучше ваших 50"
- "В Дубае нет 'bubble'. Вот данные за 5 лет"

**Trap:** Claim must be defendable in the video. Position without evidence
reads as bait.

## 5. First-person reveal

**Structure:** "Как я [did the thing]" / "Что я [learned/saw/did]"

**When to use:** the video IS a personal story — vlog, journey, learning.
NOT for guides or analysis (use formula 3 or 4 instead).

**Examples:**
- "Как я переехал в Дубай и почему ненавидел его первый год"
- "Что я узнал про Дубай за 5 лет — честно"
- "Как мы построили Core Realty с нуля до $40M GMV"

**Trap:** "Как" in Russian / "How I" in English is overused. Only use it
when the story really *is* the lesson.

## Length budget

- 50–70 characters total. YouTube cuts at ~70 on most surfaces.
- If you can't fit the formula in 70 chars — the formula is wrong for this
  video, switch to a different one.

## Forbidden patterns (auto-fail)

- "Ultimate Guide", "Complete Guide", "Everything You Need to Know"
- "The Truth About …"
- ALL CAPS anywhere
- "🤯", "🔥", "💯", "🚀" (any emoji)
- "?" as the entire end of the title (rhetorical questions)
- More than one "!"
- "Beginners Guide" / "For Dummies"
