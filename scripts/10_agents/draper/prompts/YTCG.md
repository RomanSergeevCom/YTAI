# YTCG — Connect Group overrides

## Audience voice

International entrepreneur (US/EU/Asia) doing real research before committing
$15-50K and a relocation to UAE company setup. They've already watched 30+
minutes of competitor content. They want concrete, no-BS guidance on a
specific step they're stuck on.

## Hook patterns that work

- "[Specific cost amount] — what the consultants don't tell you" — number + contrarian
- "Free Zone vs Mainland — for [specific business type]" — specificity (not generic)
- "I almost picked [wrong free zone]. Here's why I switched" — contradiction
- "[Specific free zone] interview: real costs and timelines" — credibility-by-name

## Hook patterns that fail

- "How to start a business in Dubai" — broadest possible, ranks but doesn't convert
- "UAE company setup explained" — competitors own this
- "Step by step guide" — too encyclopedia-feeling

## CTA block

```
🔗 Connect Group consultation: https://connectgroup.co/contact
🔗 Free first call (30 min): https://connectgroup.co/discovery
🔗 Subscribe — if you're seriously considering UAE/Saudi business setup.
```

## Standard hashtags

```
#UAECompanySetup #ConnectGroup #FreeZone #BusinessInDubai #UAEResidency #CorporateTax
```

## Forbidden for this brand

- "Cheap" / "Lowest cost" — Connect Group positions on accuracy and reliability,
  not lowest-bidder. Avoid framing on price-leadership.
- "Quick" / "Fast" — UAE setup has real timelines; over-promising speed
  destroys trust at first month
- "Tax-free paradise" — incorrect since 9% Corporate Tax (2023+). Use
  "tax-efficient" if needed.

## Tone notes

- Connect Group has government-partner-tier credibility — lean on that in
  description CTA, not in title (audience is allergic to authority-flexing
  in titles).
- Free Zone names are SEO-gold: IFZA, Meydan, DMCC, RAKEZ, UAQ — use them by
  name when the video references them.
- "9% Corporate Tax" / "5% VAT" / "AED 375,000 threshold" — concrete numbers
  beat "tax obligations" — pull from transcript when present.
